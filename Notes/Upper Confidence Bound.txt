Upper Confidence Bound (UCB)

Upper confidence bound is a family of algorithms in machine learning and statistics for solving the "multi-armed bandit" problem and also
addressing the exploration-exploitation trade-off. Because in machine learning, it cannot choose to both explore and exploit at the same
time. UCB selects actions by computing an upper confidence estimate for each action's potential reward, which helps balance exploration of
uncertain options with exploitation of those known to perform well.

UCB actions uses uncertainty in the action-reward estimates for balancing exploration and exploitation. Since there is inherent uncertainty
in the accuracy of the action-reward estimates when using a sample set of rewards, UCB uses uncertainty in the estimates to drive exploration.
