Artificial Neural Network (ANN)

ANN are computer systems designed to mimic how the human brain processes information. Consisting of interconnected neurons
to process data and make decisions. ANNs are used to analyze data, identify patterns and make predictions.
These layers of interconnected neurons that work together to solve complex problems. The ANN "learns" from the data it
processes, just like a human brain learns from experience.

3 Key Components of ANN:
1. Input Layer: This layer receives the initial data and passes it to the next layer for processing.
2. Hidden Layer(s): These layers perform computations on the data received from the input layer. They extract features
and patterns from the data. The number of hidden layers and neurons can vary based on the complexity of the problem.
3. Output Layer: This layer produces the final output based on the computations performed by the hidden layers.

By adjusting weights for the connections between input and hidden layers, the ANN can learn to make accurate predictions
based on the data it processes. Over time, as more data is fed into the neural network, it improves its performance.
